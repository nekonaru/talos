# Talos Brand

Talos is named after the bronze automaton who watched over Crete. The mark shows an eye whose
pupil is a download arrow: the sentinel sees what it fetches, then fetches it.

The mark is one shape. Do not redraw, recolor, re-space or split it.

## Files

| File | Use |

## Colors

| Token | Value | Use |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Talos Bronze | `#D09A5B` | The mark on dark backgrounds |
| Deep Bronze | `#9C6B30` | The mark on light backgrounds |
| Talos Near Black | `#161311` | Tile, dark background |
| Warm Paper | `#F5F1E8` | Light background |

One bronze per context. Never put the bronze mark on a bronze tile.

## Typography

Marcellus for display use (wordmark, big titles), Inter for body text. Both are
SIL Open Font License fonts with outlines constructed from the official files.

## Clear space and minimum size

Clear space equals half the mark height on every side. Minimum size is 16 px.
Below 32 px use `talos-mark-small.svg`.

## Do and do not

- Do keep the eye and arrow as one connected gesture.
- Do use one bronze per context.
- Do not recolor the mark, stretch it, or rotate it.
- Do not place it on a busy background without a solid tile behind it.
- Do not set the wordmark in any typeface other than Marcellus.
